# Mission-Scheduling Source Adapter Contract

Status: **implemented public v1 foundation**.

Source-plan schema: `oel.mission_scheduling_source_plan.v1`.

Source-evidence schema: `oel.mission_scheduling_source_evidence.v1`.

## Purpose And Boundary

The source adapter converts completed OEL optical-collection and directed-link
products into `oel.mission_scheduling_problem.v1`. It removes manual copying of
window times, data volumes, capacities, asset identities, and source hashes. It
does not create access, repair products, rerun their physics silently, or
authorize collection or communications.

Every source plan declares one Julian-date UTC epoch, one relative-seconds
scheduling horizon, the scheduler's per-asset constraints, collection-product
references, and directed-link-product references. Source IDs are unique,
portable identifiers and become part of generated opportunity IDs.
For the optional single-asset orbit-bound route, the plan also declares
`orbit_history_semantic_sha256`. Every collection source must cite this digest
as `orbit_history_semantic_sha256`, and every directed-link manifest and packet
must carry an identical `orbit_binding.parent_history_sha256`. Missing or
mismatched citations fail source conversion and retained-source replay. The
strict study inspector separately recomputes the products from the retained
history; a matching citation alone is not physical proof.

## Collection Conversion

The adapter accepts `oel.collection_opportunity_evidence.v1` JSON with
`status = completed`. It requires:

- frame/time provenance matching the source-plan epoch;
- a strictly increasing sample ledger covering the scheduling horizon;
- one unique task opportunity for every accepted candidate;
- exact agreement between candidate and task start, end, duration/objective,
  and generated data;
- matching asset identity and upstream problem digest; and
- disabled collection-local resource screening.

The last rule prevents hidden single-observation storage/downlink decisions
from being combined with the scheduler's shared multi-observation ledger. The
adapter converts generated bytes to observation data volume, retains target and
pointing evidence, applies only the source plan's explicit objective scale and
energy cost, and uses the collection evidence file's SHA-256 as the scheduling
source-product identity.

## Directed-Link Conversion

The adapter accepts a completed `oel.directed-link-analysis.v0.1` artifact
directory. It verifies every manifest artifact digest, evidence-packet and
summary identity, UTC epoch, endpoint identities, and terminal frames. Exactly
one declared endpoint must be the body-frame spacecraft and the other the
fixed-site ENU station.

Each retained link interval must lie within the scheduling horizon and satisfy:

`estimated delivered bits = interval duration * configured data rate`.

The adapter converts bits to bytes and creates one zero-objective downlink
opportunity with the source link's semantic SHA-256. Shared scheduling-station
identity is explicit in the source plan. Directed-link v0.1 does not retain an
absolute interval pointing vector, so a source plan enabling spacecraft slew
constraints must provide a normalized `pointing_unit_eci`; the adapter never
invents one.

## Retention And Replay

`build-solve` retains byte-identical copies of every source product under
`source_products/`, rewrites the normalized source plan to those portable
copies, rebuilds the scheduling problem from the retained evidence, and only
then writes the exact schedule packet. The outer manifest binds:

- the normalized retained source plan;
- every retained collection and directed-link artifact;
- upstream and scheduling source identities;
- extracted opportunity IDs; and
- the nested mission-schedule manifest and semantic identities.

`replay-sources` verifies all receipts, reparses every retained product,
rebuilds the normalized scheduling problem, compares it with the scheduled
problem, reruns the exact optimizer, and verifies the nested resource and
delivery evidence. Original source paths are not required and are not copied
into the durable manifest.

## Validation And Claims

Acceptance uses a complete deterministic OEL chain: two ONP optical collection
analyses and three directed-link analyses produce two observations, one
contended ground-station pair, and one alternate downlink. Tests verify the
hand-expected optimum and bit-to-byte capacity, then exercise artifact tamper,
candidate/task mismatch, hidden resource screening, epoch/horizon/endpoint
mismatch, source-order determinism, source-ID confinement, missing slew
pointing, manifest-claim tamper, source retention, CLI execution, and
authoritative replay.

This establishes conversion and provenance correctness for the supported OEL
products. It does not independently revalidate the physical accuracy of a
source product, infer freshness beyond its content identity and declared
epoch/horizon, or make the resulting schedule operational. No paid service is
required.

## Local Edition And Private-Service Boundary

The local edition includes this bounded adapter, self-contained source
retention, exact problem construction, deterministic example, tests, and
replay. It does not include bulk/live product ingestion, customer data
governance, rolling replanning, crosslinks and routing, uncertainty,
disruptions, operational stores, large optimization, or review-ready service
workflows. Hosted service infrastructure and the Manifold remain private.
